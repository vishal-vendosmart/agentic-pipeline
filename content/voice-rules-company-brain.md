## What carries over from the old lane
- Engineer / team = the asset. The system is the problem. Never the person.
- No product names (WeFab, ProQsmart, AI agents, "our platform") in the post body.
- No em dashes. Hyphens only.
- No markdown in social copy (`**bold**`, `_italic_`, `###`, backticks) - it renders literally.
- 180-220 words. Readable in 60 seconds.
- Max one number per post. Most posts: zero.
- No promotional language in the first two lines.
- No "Thoughts?" close.

## The story arc this lane sits on
Old lane = the "before" (the workflow is broken, the best people absorb the cost).
This lane = the "after" (the company brain exists, coworkers run on top of it, the team gets its time back).
This is not a product announcement. It is an observation of an operating model that is now possible.

## The five pillars
1. **The company brain** - decades of static data on drives. The knowledge exists; it is not connected. Not a search problem, a structure problem.
2. **Coworkers, not SaaS seats** - tool sprawl. Nine tools, and a human still moves data between them by hand. A dashboard waits; a coworker works.
3. **Elevating the team** - the nuance guardrail. Not removing jobs. Removing the computer work so people do the judgment work.
4. **Mid-market manufacturing reality** - industrial machinery, SPM, automation, packaging, material handling, process equipment. ETO/CTO/MTO. Also engineering services (India) and commercial fitout (UAE). Too complex for spreadsheets, too small for Teamcenter.
5. **Private AI / data sovereignty** - it runs on the customer's own server. Nothing leaves their environment. They own and control the model and the memory. The differentiator against every cloud AI vendor selling into manufacturing. Not a feature - the reason to choose them.

## Close shapes (vary; no two consecutive the same)

- **D - Reframing:** name what the company calls the problem vs. what it actually is.

## Banned
- "A shop I visited", "I walked through a factory", "I sat in a room with", "I have seen this in" - all witness-authority openers.
- "instead of", "rather than", "wasting time on" (when applied to the person as cost).
- "clerical", "data entry", "low-skill", "low-judgment".
- Salary framing ("$140k engineer doing X").
- Named personas (no Sarah, no "the engineer at Acme").
- Fictional specific moments (no "4:15 PM Tuesday", no "over coffee").
