## What this lane is (locked tonight)

The company builds **private AI on a company's own knowledge, running on the customer's own server**. The deliverable unit is an **agentic coworker** that maps to a role/JD. Proposal engineering is ONE coworker - proof of the model. **We are not a proposals company.** Never position that way.

Vishal's own vote on the mix: **problem-lead hook + category insight + a Japanese packaging line builder** as the proof anchor.

## The 3 posts

1. **Problem-led + the anchor.** Open on the problem the company has (expertise is real, memory is broken; decades of engineering sitting in people's heads and dead drives). Use the Japanese packaging line builder as the concrete illustration. Category insight lands at the end, not the start.
2. **Category insight.** The unit of value is a coworker, not another dashboard/seat. Tool sprawl: nine systems, and a human still moves the data between them by hand. A dashboard waits; a coworker works.
3. **Private AI / sovereignty, still problem-led.** Open on why this kind of company will not put drawings, costing, and customer work into someone else's cloud. Land on: their AI, on their server, nothing leaves, they control it.

## Hard constraints (non-negotiable)

- 180-220 words each. Readable in 60 seconds.
- Problem-led hook. No promotional language in the first two lines. No product names in the body (no WeFab, Webot, "our platform", "AI agents" as a brand).
- **No em dashes.** Hyphens only.
- **No markdown** in the social copy (`**bold**`, `_italic_`, `#`, backticks render literally).
- Max one number per post. Most posts: zero.
- Do not call it a proposals company, a proposal tool, or an RFQ product. Do not use "RFQ", "proposal engineer", or "get X out the door" in openers.
- Anchor rule: refer to it as "a Japanese packaging line builder". Do NOT name the company, do NOT claim a partnership, do NOT invent internal facts, numbers, or quotes about it. Only structural truths that hold for any built-to-order packaging machine builder.
- Close with one of the four close shapes in the voice file. Vary them; no two consecutive the same. No "Thoughts?".
- Engineer/team = the asset. The system is the problem. Never the person.
