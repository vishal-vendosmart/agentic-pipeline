## Blog Draft
# The RFQ Gap

Who owns the RFQ package?

Most companies think they do.
They are wrong.

Engineers focus on the design.
Sourcing managers focus on the cost and the timeline.

Neither is the "package person."

There is a gap.
The BOM doesn't match the drawing.
The spec doesn't match the cert.

This is not a personnel problem.
It is a structural problem.

No one is hired to assemble the package.

When this happens, the work moves to the vendor.
The vendor begins to organize the files.
The vendor becomes the project manager for the buyer.

You give away control.

The solution is simple: ownership.
You need a dedicated owner for this step.
One person ensures the package is organized and accurate.

If you don't have an owner, your vendor is the owner.
And they are doing it for their own benefit, not yours.

Who in your company actually assembles the RFQ package?
