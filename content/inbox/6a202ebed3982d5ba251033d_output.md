## Blog Draft
# The RFQ Puzzle

You send an RFQ.
You include 8 files and 32 drawings.
You leave the links between them for the vendor to find.

You think you're asking for a quote.
You're actually giving them a puzzle.

Vendors spend hours matching BOM line 17 to drawing sheet 3.
They chase you for missing files.
They become the project manager for your own request.

This work is invisible.
It does not appear on the invoice.

Instead, it is absorbed into the price.
Or worse, it leads to a quote based on a guess.

I once got the "best" bid from a vendor who simply guessed the best.

The alternative is the organized package.
The buyer manages. The vendor executes.

Check your RFQs.
Are you asking for a price, or are you testing their ability to solve a puzzle?
