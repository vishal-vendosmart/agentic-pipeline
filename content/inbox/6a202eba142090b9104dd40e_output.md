## Blog Draft
# The Hidden Cost of the Incomplete RFQ

The real cost of an incomplete RFQ isn't the vendor's confusion.
It's your time.

I used to send RFQs that were incomplete.
I thought it was a minor issue.
It wasn't.

I spent 14 hours a month chasing my own packages.
I hunted for the latest drawing revision across five email threads.
Often, I found two versions and neither was the final one.

I spent an hour asking engineering to clarify a spec.
The spec should have been on the drawing.
I spent thirty minutes attaching files, only to realize I forgot the test requirements.

I had calls with vendors that lasted 45 minutes.
Why? Because the BOM contradicted the drawing.

None of those hours were sourcing.
They were clerical work.
I had offloaded the administrative burden to myself.

The fix is discipline.
Organize the package once.
Write everything down before sending.

Run an audit on your own RFQs.
It takes 15 minutes.

Ask yourself: How many hours do I spend chasing my own RFQ package before it even leaves my desk?
