I tracked a project across 4 spreadsheets once.

Not because I liked spreadsheets. Because nobody built a tool for custom machine tracking.

Spreadsheet 1: Parts ordered. Vendor. Cost. Due date.
Spreadsheet 2: Parts received. Serial numbers. QC status.
Spreadsheet 3: Assembly progress. Issues found. Fixes needed.
Spreadsheet 4: Shipping schedule. Customs docs. Delivery confirmation.

The problem was not data. It was the seams between spreadsheets.

Vendor #3 delayed a part. I updated Spreadsheet 1. Forgot to update Spreadsheet 3. Assembly team waited 5 days for a part that was delayed, but nobody told them.

I thought about building software to fix this. Then I remembered: I am a machine builder, not a software company.

So I kept using spreadsheets. And kept missing updates.

The best tool is the one you actually use. But when your process is broken, better tools just help you fail faster.
