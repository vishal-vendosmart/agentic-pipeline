I have reviewed hundreds of manufacturing contracts.

The ones that fail have a pattern.

The client asks: Can you handle assembly?

The vendor says yes. But they mean "we can machine parts." Machining is not assembly.

I have seen contracts where 47 parts arrive on time. Each meets spec. But nobody checked if they fit together.

The tolerance stack-up? Discovered at assembly. The interference? Found during install. The wiring harness? 40mm short because Vendor A measured differently than Vendor B.

The contract did not fail because of bad parts. It failed because nobody owned the integration.

The best contracts I see have one line: "Vendor owns delivered machine, not just delivered parts."

That line changes everything.
