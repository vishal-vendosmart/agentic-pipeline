"We'll assemble as parts arrive." - The parallel assembly myth.

Sounds efficient. Start assembly with partial kit. Build what you have. Add parts as they come.

Reality:

Part 1 arrives. Start assembly. Need Part 7 to continue. Not here yet.

Part 7 arrives 2 weeks later. But Part 1 was built assuming Part 7 would be somewhere else. Rework.

Part 12 arrives. Doesn't fit with Part 3 because Part 3 was installed without Part 12's mounting bracket. More rework.

By the time all 47 parts arrive, you've built the machine 1.5 times.

Parallel assembly works for LEGO. Not for custom machines with tolerance stack-up.

Single partner model: parts arrive together. Assembly sequence is planned. No surprises. No rework.

One kit. One build. One machine.
