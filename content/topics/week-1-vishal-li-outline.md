## Post 1: The 4:15 PM Export Queue
- **Pillar:** A (Day-in-the-life / repetitive work)
- **Account:** Vishal personal LinkedIn
- **Scheduled:** Wed Jul 15, 8:00 AM IST
- **Hook (gut-punch):** "Most engineers came here to do great work. The workflow eats their time and energy before they get a chance."
- **Body direction:** Founder observation of a pattern seen in custom machinery shops. Senior engineers spending late afternoons on repetitive 2D drawing conversions, losing energy for the actual design work. The workflow puts the clerical work first. The hard work waits.
- **Closing question (locked):** "If you have ever felt your best ideas slip away because the export queue came first, you are not alone."
- **Pain mechanism (no fictional specifics):** Workflow orders tasks by what is easy to track, not by what creates value. Engineers end up doing cheap work first because the system has no rule for ordering by impact.

## Post 2: The Empty Handover
- **Pillar:** C (Knowledge graveyard)
- **Account:** Vishal personal LinkedIn
- **Scheduled:** Thu Jul 16, 8:00 AM IST
- **Hook (gut-punch):** "The cost of one retirement in a custom shop is often 30% of throughput. Twenty years of institutional memory gone in a week."
- **Body direction:** Founder observation of the retirement problem across SPM shops, packaging lines, industrial automation builders. Senior engineer retires, handover doc is empty, line is behind schedule by Wednesday, company blames the new hire. The workflow was built around the assumption the person would always be there.
- **Closing question (locked):** "If you have ever watched a colleague retire and wondered what you will do without the answers they carried in their head, you are not alone."
- **Pain mechanism (no fictional specifics):** Knowledge that was never written down because it never had to be. When the person leaves, the knowledge leaves with them. The company has no system to capture it before it walks out the door.

- **Pillar:** A (Design review)
- **Account:** Vishal personal LinkedIn
- **Scheduled:** Fri Jul 17, 8:00 AM IST
- **Hook (gut-punch):** "Four senior leads. Two hours. One bolt. Zero decisions."
- **Body direction:** Founder observation of the design review meeting pattern. No system for small decisions means every tolerance call becomes a meeting. Four expensive minds in a room, schedule slipping, real design work waiting in the hallway.

## Post 4: The Friday Apology Email
- **Pillar:** B (Cost of slow quotes)
- **Account:** Vishal personal LinkedIn
- **Scheduled:** Sat Jul 18, 8:00 AM IST
- **Hook (gut-punch):** "The lead was hot on Tuesday. By Thursday the deal was gone. The engineers were doing the work. The system around the work was what was slow."
- **Body direction:** Founder observation of the slow-quote pattern from the sales side. The system turned their hard work into a bottleneck.
