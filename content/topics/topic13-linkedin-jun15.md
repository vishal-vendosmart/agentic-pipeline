From CAD to first power-up: what actually happens in custom machine building.

Week 0: CAD complete. You feel good.

Week 1: RFQs to 12 vendors. 8 respond. 4 ask for "more clarity." 2 quote random numbers that make no sense.

Week 4: Finally have quotes. Start negotiating. Realize Vendor #3 forgot to include tooling costs.

Week 6: Place orders. Spread across 8 vendors. Start tracking 8 different lead times.

Week 12: Parts start arriving. Vendor #7 is 2 weeks late. Vendor #5 shipped wrong revision. Rework #1.

Week 14: Begin assembly. Bracket doesn't fit frame. Tolerance stack-up. Rework #2.

Week 16: Electronics enclosure clashes with pneumatic routing. Did anyone check clearances? Rework #3.

Week 18: First power-on. Something shorts. Root cause: Vendor #9's cable routing violated spec. But their drawing was "approved."

Week 20: Fixed. Tested. Running. 5 months after planned delivery.

Week 10: Assembly starts. Techs who understand the full machine.
Week 12: First power-on. Works. Because tolerance stack-up was managed from day one.
