"We can machine that." - Every vendor, ever.

But here's what they don't tell you: machining your bracket and assembling your machine are two completely different capabilities.

I learned this the hard way on a climate control system project. Vendor A machined beautiful aluminum housings. Within spec. Perfect surface finish. Shipped on time.

Vendor B built the electronics enclosure. Also perfect. Also on time.

But when we assembled them? The thermal interface didn't mate. The mounting holes were 0.5mm off. The cable routing clashed with a structural rib that neither vendor knew existed because neither saw the full assembly.

3 weeks of rework. $15K in scrap. 2 engineers flown on-site to hand-file parts.

The vendor who says "yes" to your drawing but asks zero questions about assembly is more dangerous than the one who says "we need to talk about how this fits."

Machining != assembly.
Sheet metal != integration.
Testing != validation.

The best vendors ask about your machine, not just their part.

Ever had parts that were "within spec" but didn't assemble?
