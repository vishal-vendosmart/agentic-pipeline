I watched a competitor promise delivery in 12 weeks. They shipped in 28.

Not because their factories were worse. Because their system was broken.

They had good engineers. Good vendors. Good intentions.

What they did not have: a single throat to choke.

Vendor A machined brackets. Vendor B cut housings. Vendor C wired electronics.

Each delivered on time. Each met spec.

But when the assembly team tried to put them together? The bracket interfered with the housing. The wiring harness was 40mm too short.

Nobody owned the integration. So nobody fixed it until Month 7.

I learned that custom machines are not about better parts. They are about who owns the outcome when parts do not fit.

Have you had vendors deliver "on time" but the machine still shipped late?
