You pay a senior engineer $140k to act as a human copy-paste machine.

They spent six years mastering thermodynamics. Now they spend six hours a day clicking Export.

They move data from a CAD screen to a spreadsheet. One cell at a time.

It is not engineering. It is data entry.

The cost is not just the salary. It is the burnout.

The best minds in your shop are bored. They are doing the work of a clerk.

When the real design problems hit, they are too tired to solve them.

You do not have a capacity problem. You have a clerical problem.

Maybe your engineers should actually engineer.
