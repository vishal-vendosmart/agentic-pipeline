From 47 vendors to 1: how an automation integrator cut assembly time by 60%.

Before:
- 47 separate vendors for one custom line
- 6 months of assembly (planned: 8 weeks)
- 3 rework loops from tolerance stack-up
- 2 engineers managing vendors full-time
- Endless email threads: "Is the bracket ready?" "What's the status on the enclosure?"

After switching to a single AI-native partner:
- 1 partner. One dashboard. One throat to choke.
- 2.5 months assembly
- Zero rework loops (they owned tolerance stack-up from day one)
- Engineers focused on design, not vendor management
- Real-time tracking: "Your enclosure is in QC. Assembly starts Tuesday."

The machine didn't change. The parts didn't get cheaper. The assembly process got an owner.

This is what happens when someone takes accountability for the entire build, not just their 3 components.

47 vendors vs. 1 partner. Same machine. 60% faster.

The difference wasn't better parts. It was single-point accountability.
