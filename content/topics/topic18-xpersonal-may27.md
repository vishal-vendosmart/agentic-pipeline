"We can machine that." - Every vendor, ever.

Machining your bracket and assembling your machine are two completely different capabilities.

Vendor A machined beautiful housings. Within spec. Perfect finish. On time.

Vendor B built the enclosure. Also perfect. Also on time.

Assembly? Thermal interface didn't mate. Holes 0.5mm off. Cable routing clashed with a rib neither vendor knew existed.

3 weeks rework. $15K scrap. 2 engineers flown on-site to hand-file parts.

The vendor who says "yes" but asks zero questions about assembly is more dangerous than the one who says "we need to talk about how this fits."
