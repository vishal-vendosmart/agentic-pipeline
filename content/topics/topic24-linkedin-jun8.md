The $50K part that should have cost $5K.

A robotics company needed a precision gear housing. Tight tolerances. Special alloy. Complex geometry.

Vendor A quoted $50K. "CNC time. Material cost. Special tooling."

Vendor B quoted $5K. Same drawing. Same specs.

The difference? Vendor A saw a complex part and quoted what they knew. Vendor B saw a complex part and asked "can we cast near-net and finish with CNC?"

Different manufacturing strategy. Same result. 10x cost difference.

This is why AI DFM matters. It doesn't just check manufacturability. It suggests alternative processes. "This geometry is ideal for investment casting + finish machining. Estimated cost: $5K vs. $50K."

A good manufacturing partner thinks about process, not just price. A great one has AI that thinks about it first.
