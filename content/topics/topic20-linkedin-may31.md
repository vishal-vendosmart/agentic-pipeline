The assembly instruction problem.

Your design team spent 6 months on CAD. FEA. Simulations. Perfect design.

Then they handed a 50-page PDF to the assembly team.

"Assemble per drawing."

But the drawing shows the latest revision. The parts were ordered from revision 3. Two changes happened since.

The assembly tech doesn't know about the cable routing change in rev 5. Or the bracket orientation update in rev 6. Because they weren't in the meetings.

So they guess. And sometimes they're wrong.

The gap between design intent and assembly reality is measured in PDF revisions. And every gap is a potential failure.

Single partner model: design and assembly teams work together from day one. Same system. Same CAD. Same truth.

No guessing.
