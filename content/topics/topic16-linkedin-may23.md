The 3 AM vendor email. You know the one.

"Sorry for delay. The bracket is almost ready. We had issue with fixture. Will ship next week."

You read it at 7 AM. Your standup is at 9. The customer asked for status yesterday. And "next week" was 2 weeks ago.

This is custom manufacturing. The timezone gap between you and your 12 vendors is not 8 hours. It's infinite. Because communication is asynchronous when you need it synchronous.

You can't fly to every factory for every delay. You can't call at 3 AM. You can't scream over email. So you wait. And your project slips another week.

The real cost of multi-vendor projects? It's not the parts. It's the emotional tax of chasing people who don't feel your urgency.

Single partner, one timezone, one accountability point. That's how you sleep through the night.
