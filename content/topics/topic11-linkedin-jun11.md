I watched a talented team sit idle for 18 months.

Not because they lacked ideas. They had pages of concepts. Sketches. Market research.

Their internal team had world-class engineers. Mechanical design. Controls. Assembly.

But they were tied up with current projects. Running existing lines. Supporting customers. Firefighting.

New product innovation sat on the shelf. Not because it was bad. Because there was nobody left to build it.

I have seen this in automation, robotics, climate tech. The same pattern.

The best engineers are always busy. The new ideas always wait. And the market moves on.

I thought the solution was hiring more engineers. But hiring takes 6 months. Training takes 3. By then, the opportunity is gone.

The real bottleneck is not talent. It is bandwidth.

How do you free up engineering time for new development?
