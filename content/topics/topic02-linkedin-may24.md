Before you sign that manufacturing contract for your custom automation line, ask these 3 questions. The wrong answers will cost you 6 months and $200K in delays.

Question 1: Do you handle assembly, or just manufacture parts?

Most vendors say "yes" to everything. But "we can machine that" doesn't mean "we can assemble your machine." Machining != assembly. Sheet metal != integration. Testing != validation. If they don't have an assembly floor with experienced techs, you're still managing that yourself.

Question 2: Who owns tolerance stack-up when parts don't fit?

47 parts from 12 vendors. Each within spec. Assembled? Misalignment, interference, backlash. If every vendor just ships to their own drawing, nobody owns the stack-up. You need one partner who says "this is my problem" when parts don't interface.

Question 3: Can you build from my CAD directly, without 6 months of back-and-forth?

Some manufacturers need 20 revision cycles to understand your design. Others have AI DFM that reads your CAD and flags manufacturability issues in hours, not weeks. The difference? 4 months of RFQ time vs. 4 days.

The best manufacturing partner isn't the cheapest. It's the one who takes accountability for the entire machine, not just their 3 parts.
