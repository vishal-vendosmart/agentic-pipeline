# AI Employee Email Sequence - v4 (Presence & Autonomy)

## Email 1: The Operational Lead
**Subject:** Computer work

Most of your day is spent doing "computer work."

Filling out permits. Updating procurement logs. Drafting documentation.
It is tedious. It takes hours.

We build AI agents that handle these operations end-to-end.

This is not a new piece of software to learn. 
It is a presence that lives on your server.

It does the work and reports the results to you via Slack, Teams, or email.

It handles the procurement. It files the permits. It writes the docs.
You just get the notification that it is done.

Do any of these sound like the computer work that's slowing you down?

## Email 2: The Visualization
**Subject:** Not a tool

Most AI software is just another tool.

It gives you a dashboard. You log in. You give it instructions. You manage the output.
That is just more work.

Our agents are different.

They are not tools you operate. They are capabilities that work alongside you.

The agent lives on your server. It uses your data. It stays in your environment.

It does not ask you to check a dashboard.
It updates your CRM. It manages your project tools. It handles the heavy documentation.

Then it sends you a message in Slack or Teams.
"The procurement log is updated. The permits are filed."

It is a teammate that delivers the result, not a tool that requires your attention.

Do you have a specific operational bottleneck we could map out in a brief chat?
