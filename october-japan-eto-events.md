## 🛠️ Execution Strategies

### 1. "The Fishing Strategy" (When You Exhibit)
**Target:** DX Managers, IT Directors, Innovation Leads.
- **Booth Messaging:** Move away from "AI Agents" $\rightarrow$ **"Enterprise ETO Automation: Reduce Lead-to-Cash Cycle for Custom Machinery."**
- **Goal:** Capture the "Solution Buyer" who has a budget for Digital Transformation.

### 2. "The Hunting Strategy" (When They Exhibit)
**Target:** Technical Directors, VP of Operations, Engineering Heads.
- **The Barrier:** Sales reps often block access to decision-makers.
- **The Hook:** **"AI Proposal Engineering."**
- **The Script:** *"I'm not selling generic AI. I've built an AI Proposal Engineer that automates the technical spec process so your sales team doesn't have to wait on your engineers to close a deal. Who handles the technical bottleneck in your quoting process?"*

### 3. The "Margin Recovery" Pitch (For the $100M+ C-Suite)
When speaking to the decision-maker, pivot from "efficiency" to **"Profitability."**
- **Key Angle:** Every day a custom quote sits with an engineer is **lost margin**.
- **Value Prop:** AI Proposal Engineers remove the engineering bottleneck, allowing senior engineers to focus on design while sales closes deals in hours, not weeks.
