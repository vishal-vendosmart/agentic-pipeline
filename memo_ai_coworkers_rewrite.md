**The idea**

AI coworkers are digital team members. They are built on your company's own knowledge. They do the work your people do on computers - reading, reconciling, drafting, and tracking. 

They are supervised by your people. They are under your control.

They are not chatbots that answer questions. They are coworkers that work and report to a human.

**The foundation**

Your company has a lot of data. Sales records, accounts, emails, and approvals. This is your institutional memory.

We turn that data into a living map. Every project, customer, and payment is connected. You can find any answer in seconds.

You do not need to move your data or enter new information. We build the map from what you already have. Once the map exists, the coworkers can function.

**What an AI coworker is**

Onboarding takes weeks, not months. They read your history and your ways of working.

They know every project and every commitment. They never forget or confuse one project with another.

They execute real tasks. They compile, reconcile, and assemble. They work 24/7, even during surges.

They report to a supervisor. They do not take leave or ask for raises.

Your people stop doing the repetitive computer work. They move to supervising it. They review, approve, and decide.

These are illustrations, not a menu. The real list comes from you.

Sales and bookings: Capture leads from WhatsApp, track follow-ups, and maintain a live funnel. No lead slips through.

Collections: Maintain payment schedules and reconcile accounts. You get cash flow visibility before the bank meeting, not after.

This is all repetitive computer work. It is valuable, but easy to let slip. 

AI coworkers do it consistently. Every claim is traced to a source. Your people do the judging and deciding. That is the part only humans can do.

Each coworker reports to a human on your team.
